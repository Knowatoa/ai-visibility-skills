# Example: a storyboard checked against the stage

Talk: "ARE We F*cked? An Anti-AI-Doomer's Perspective." 40 minutes. The storyboard had nine beats and said "Can we lock this outline: **Yes**." This is how each beat did in the rehearsal transcript.

| # | Beat | What it depended on | Should have been marked | On stage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The waffle laugh | Its own story | yes | ✅ Landed |
| 2 | The doom, named (deliberate quiet) | Withholding the answers to the fears until later blocks | fragile: held-back payoffs | ❌ Fears answered on the spot, brisk, not quiet |
| 3 | The middle has receipts | Its own numbers | yes | ✅ Landed, earlier than planned |
| 4 | That's me (arguing with the AI) | Its own story | yes | ✅ Strongest moment |
| 5 | Rules I can steal | Four named pattern pairs | fragile: too many named parts | ❌ Pairs collapsed into three moves |
| 6 | Permission to log off | A secondhand story from X, plus a contrast with beat 8 | fragile: secondhand proof, neighbor contrast | 🟡 Feeling survived through new lived stories |
| 7 | The homework, done (the stack) | Same energy as beat 5 | merge with 5 | ❌ Absorbed into the core |
| 8 | The waffles come back | A callback to minute 2, held until minute 30 | fragile: long-range callback | ❌ Happened at minute 9 with no callback |
| 9 | The reveal and the send-off | Holding the reveal for the close | fragile: held-back reveal | 🟡 Reveal came mid-talk; the close still worked |

Every beat marked `yes` landed. Every fragile beat drifted. The Leave-them idea depended on beat 5, so the honest lock line was:

> **Not yet.** The Monday takeaway rides on beat 5's four named pairs, which is too many to carry on stage. The self-contained version is beat 4's arguing test on its own.
