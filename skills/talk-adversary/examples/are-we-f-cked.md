# Example: drift map, rewrite, and Next time

Talk: "ARE We F*cked? An Anti-AI-Doomer's Perspective." 40-minute slot. Inputs: outline, storyboard, slides plan, and a rehearsal transcript with timestamps.

## What went wrong in the first adversary pass

The first pass found good holes. Then it **appended** "Aug 27 run-through" paragraphs next to the planned blocks and kept the dropped material as "source material." The outline grew to ~4,000 words describing two talks at once. One write-back also put back a device the speaker had dropped ("Two of these are noise. One has teeth."), and nothing labeled it as a restore.

The version below is what the skill should produce instead.

## A rewritten block (spoken spine, not appended notes)

> **Own your most important job** (14:15–19:00, ~5 min)
> If you keep arguing with the AI about a job, that job is yours: you're more opinionated about it than the machine is. For me that's customers: interviews, support tickets, feature requests. Claude processes the interview transcript; what to do next comes from the conversation I was in.

## Cut (planned, not spoken)

- Right-size the workspace / the everything-chat: not said; candidate example under "hand off the rest."
- Make them compete (multi-model arena): not said.
- The X story about suing an AI lab over usage limits: not said; secondhand and unverified.
- Phones-out live check of what ChatGPT says about your category: not said.

## Holes (labeled)

- **"AI will take my job" answered with adoption stats** (04:30–06:15). Stuck question: "early doesn't mean safe." Bridge to the own-your-job section in one sentence. `adopt-spoken` → block "Fears," slide 10 notes.
- **SEO fear conceded, then the company pitch** (08:30–11:30). Criticism: the one fear the speaker's company sells against is the one conceded. Say the bias out loud. `adopt-spoken` → block "Fears."
- **Promise before the fears: "Two are noise. One has teeth."** `restore-plan`: without it, the concession reads as a sales setup. One line, no deferred payoffs.

## Next time

- The outline had eight named patterns; the stage used one noun and three moves. Next outline: find the speaker's organizing word first and cap the spine at three.
- Secondhand proof (a tweet, a video) was dropped; lived stories from that week replaced it. Next outline: lived proof only, and leave a slot for this week's story.
- Payoffs deferred across the talk were answered on the spot instead. Next outline: resolve in the same block.
- The storyboard said "lock: yes" while four of nine beats depended on held-back payoffs or callbacks. Next storyboard: mark those fragile.
- 36 planned became 40 spoken. Next outline: plan to 85% of the slot.
