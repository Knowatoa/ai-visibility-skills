# Example: cues card, no slides, no recording

Talk: "He Restores My Soul," a 30–40 minute sermon at a local church. Names and places are removed.

## How the talk got here

1. Outline: seven short scratch rounds, then a locked outline built on 1 Corinthians 13.
2. Storyboard: started, not finished.
3. Slides: skipped for now. The speaker wrote a one-page cues card from the outline and practiced from it instead (~19.5 minutes, transcript pasted in chat).
4. First adversary pass: against that rehearsal transcript. The 1 Corinthians frame was dropped in favor of Psalm 23, which is what the speaker kept saying.
5. Delivered the next day from the printed card, changed in pen as they went. No recording.
6. Second adversary pass: the marked-up card was the spoken version. Slides come later, for the next time the talk is given, built from the rewritten outline.

Rehearsing from cards changed the talk more than the storyboard did, and it cost nothing to throw away.

## Drift

Planned = the outline after the rehearsal. Spoken = the cues card plus pen edits.

| Planned block (min) | Spoken (~min, card) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Open (1) | Back as head deacon, serving with love (4) | kept |
| 1. The problem (2.5) | + "the world tells us we can do everything"; the heart is *hard* (8) | kept, reframed |
| 2. Transition: a moment, a week, a season (0.6) | Withdraw, rest, surrender | replaced: time spans → verbs |
| 2. In a moment (3) | Withdraw: Luke 5:16, the kids bickering | kept, renamed |
| 2. Every week, Exodus 31:17 (2.5) | Rest: Genesis 2:2 | kept; verse replaced |
| 2. In a season (5) | Surrender: Elijah, wind / earthquake / fire, the year away | kept; new emphasis on time |

| 3. Back serving (3.5) | Full cup: "who's hauling the ladder?", the ask (4) | kept |

Rehearsal ~19.5 min; card plan ~33. No recording, so timings are the card's.

## The cues card, before and after

Before (what was printed):

```markdown
## 2 · MY CUP RUNS OVER · 17 min
- Anchor: → Three ways to **let God restore you**: a moment, a week, a season

**a · A MOMENT · BOUNDARIES**
- Read **Luke 5:16**: "So He Himself often withdrew into the wilderness and prayed."
- *Often* = a habit. Walk away
- Kids bickering → what I actually do
```

After (pen edits folded in, write-backs added as one line each):

```markdown
## 2 · MY CUP RUNS OVER · 17 min
- Anchor: → Three ways to **let God restore you**: withdraw, rest, surrender

**a · WITHDRAW**
- Read **Luke 5:16**: "So He Himself often withdrew into the wilderness and prayed."
- v15: crowds biggest → that's when He withdrew
- *Often* = a habit. Walk away
- Kids bickering → what I actually do
- This week: name it, step out, pray, go back in
```

The two new lines are cues, not sentences. The full point sits in the outline block.

## A rewritten block

> **Withdraw, when it's hardening.**
> Luke 5:16, "So He Himself often withdrew into the wilderness and prayed." Look at verse 15: the crowds were the biggest they had been. That's when He withdrew. *Often* means a habit, not an emergency. When the kids bicker while we clean the kitchen, the annoyance bleeds in and I want to control it. That's my heart starting to harden: step out, breathe, pray. This week, when you feel it (the sharp tone, the keeping score), name it, step out, and pray before you go back in.

## Cut (planned, not spoken)

- The wedding open and 1 Corinthians 13 as the frame: replaced by Psalm 23 and who I am.
- "A moment, a week, a season": replaced by withdraw, rest, surrender.
- Exodus 31:17: replaced by Genesis 2:2.
- Reading 1 Kings 19:7 aloud: felt clunky when spoken; the food is now one line of story.

## Holes (labeled)

- **Withdraw has no explanation.** "Often = a habit" with no context. Luke 5:15 shows *when* He withdrew. `adopt-spoken` → outline Withdraw; storyboard beat 4; cues section 2a.
- **"Surrender takes time" is said, not shown.** The wind, earthquake, and fire carried it on the day; the explanation now sits on those verses. `adopt-spoken` → outline Surrender; storyboard beat 6; cues section 2c.
- **No this-week step in three of four sections.** One line each, all on the card. `adopt-spoken` → outline 1, 2; cues sections 1, 2a–c.

## Next time

- Pen edits turned time spans into verbs, and listeners repeated two of the verbs back afterward. Next outline: name each move as something the listener does.
- The through-line (hard, hardening, softening) showed up while speaking, not in the files. Next outline: pick the through-line image alongside the key quote.
- The final version lived only in pen. Next time: put pen edits into the cues `.md` the same day, so the next run starts from what was said.
- Practicing from cards before slides changed the frame, the verbs, and a verse, and none of it cost a slide rebuild. Next talk: cues and a rehearsal before slides.
