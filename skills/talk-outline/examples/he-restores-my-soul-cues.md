# Example: a cues card

Talk: "He Restores My Soul," a 30–40 minute sermon at a local church. Names and places are removed. The speaker rehearsed from this card, then delivered from it, before any slides existed. This is the card after the speaker's pen edits from the day were copied back into it, shortened.

```markdown
# HE RESTORES MY SOUL · ~33 min

**Key verse · Psalm 23:2–3**: "He makes me to lie down in green pastures; He leads me beside the still waters. He restores my soul."

## OPEN · 4 min
- Back as head deacon
- Serving with love

## 1 · THE PROBLEM · 8 min
- The world tells us we can do everything
- Ladder in the rain (fire marshal, HVAC, leaks)
- Not how I wanted to feel about my role
- 1 Cor 13:3 "…but have not love, it profits me nothing."
- → **"Resentment is the enemy of love."**
- My heart: hard, hardening, or softening? Yours?

## 2 · THREE WAYS · 17 min
- Not selfish: He *makes* me lie down
- → Three ways to **let God restore you**: withdraw, rest, surrender

**a · WITHDRAW**
- Read **Luke 5:16**
- v15: crowds biggest → that's when He withdrew
- Kids bickering → what I actually do

**b · REST**
- **Genesis 2:2**
- Work from home → "why throw more at me?"
- The phone brick

**c · SURRENDER**
- Elijah after Carmel, runs (1 Kings 19:4)
- Read **v11–12**: wind, earthquake, fire, still small voice
- My year away
- Stepped back, not out

## 3 · FULL CUP · 4 min
- The HVAC call → my Elisha
- Who's hauling the ladder? **Be someone's Elisha**
- **Withdraw · Rest · Surrender**
- *Rest this Sabbath. Come tell me how God restores you.*
```

What makes it a cues card and not a script:

- Every heading is an outline block with its minutes.
- Stories appear by name ("ladder in the rain," "my year away"). The speaker tells them; the card only reminds.
- Quotes to be read word for word are on the card, so the speaker doesn't paraphrase them wrong.
- The spine (withdraw, rest, surrender) appears where it is introduced and again in the close.

The printed card said "a moment, a week, a season." The speaker changed it to "withdraw, rest, surrender" in pen while delivering it. That marked-up card, not the outline, was the input for the next revision.
