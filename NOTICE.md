The way skills in this repo behave — write the deliverable as a file in
the working directory, stay self-contained, stay action-oriented, and
keep extra skills that pressure-test the other skills — is inspired by
Jason Cohen's public catalog:

- **Project:** [asb-skills](https://github.com/asmartbear/asb-skills)

His catalog implements frameworks from [A Smart Bear](https://longform.asmartbear.com)
and *[Hidden Multipliers](https://hiddenmultipliers.com)*. Those
frameworks, and the text of his `asb-*` skills, are his work. They are
not copied here.

This repo is about AI visibility. The overlap is *how* a skill should
run, not *what* it teaches.

### What we took as inspiration (rewritten in our own words)

- A skill that produces something lasting writes a markdown file on disk
  and treats that file as memory across sessions.
- The skill asks where those files should live. It does not silently
  scatter them.
- Public skills are self-contained. They do not require the rest of this
  repo, and they do not drag in extra context they can do without.
- Authoring skills exist to interrogate other skills: a static audit and
  a simulated-use exercise, not just a prose review of the markdown.

### What we did not take

- No `SKILL.md` body, archetype skeleton, wrapper page, or workshop
  sequence from asb-skills is reproduced here.
- No framework from *Hidden Multipliers* or A Smart Bear articles
  (Carol, voters, positioning, willingness-to-pay, Rude Q&A, the
  interview method, and the rest) is restated here.
